Website analytics privacy

GEO Learning Log uses optional Google Analytics to understand which reports are read and how visitors arrive. Reports are available without analytics.

If you allow analytics, Google receives page-view information, including the report address, recognized campaign labels, the referring site when available, and standard browser measurement data. Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. This site does not send a visitor name, email address or account ID to Google Analytics, and the tag disables advertising personalization and Google signals.

You can change your choice using “Analytics settings” on a measured page. We also respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
