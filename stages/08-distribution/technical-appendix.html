Measurement rules

  1. Distribution action. Count a published post or reply, delivered form, open PR, or confirmed queue slot.
  2. Platform engagement. Save platform counts and separate clicks from the broader engagement total.
  3. Website evidence. Record source-bearing sessions and eligible document requests separately. A placement label alone leaves the origin unconfirmed.
  4. Resource usage signal. Count file_download as a click event after excluding marked QA and known diagnostics. Repeated events and actual use of the file require separate evidence.
  5. Independent pickup. Check the live public entry and its link. A receipt, bot review and queued date describe stages of submission.
  6. AI visibility. NOT_MEASURED in this lesson.

To repeat the audit, keep dated Post Analytics counts, check each PR and entry at the cutoff, and use GA4 acquisition, landing pages and events by campaign. Reconcile QA against test evidence, preserve missing values, and retain owner-only raw data privately.
