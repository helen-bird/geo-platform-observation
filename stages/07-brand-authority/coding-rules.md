# GEO Lab #07 · Coding rules

- **Unit:** A creator in one selected answer, counted at most once for an outcome. The answer stays in the denominator even if no creator is recommended. Keep interface and question separate.
- **Mention:** An identifiable person or stable byline appears in the answer body. A neutral description, supporting reference or explicit exclusion can count.
- **Recommendation:** The body offers the named person as a substantive option for the user's task, including clear secondary recommendations. A byline, background reference, cited work or exclusion alone does not count.
- **Work and attribution:** Record the offered work independently of the person. Match attribution only if the answer explicitly links the person to the work and verified ownership or the recorded entity mapping supports it. Missing or uncertain mappings are not wrong attributions.
- **Sources:** Keep visible final citations, other body links, source panels and independently located evidence in separate fields. Where the needed evidence was not captured, record unknown.

The recommendation reason is a second observation, coded only on positive personal recommendations. Multiple labels may apply:

| Label | What qualifies |
| --- | --- |
| IDENTITY | A specific role or identity is given as a reason. |
| EXPERTISE | Specific experience, skills or credentials. |
| ORIGINAL_EVIDENCE | Original experiment, measurement, analysis or data. |
| TOPICAL_AUTHORITY | Sustained or systematic work in the topic. |
| THIRD_PARTY_VALIDATION | A claim of outside recognition, use or corroboration. |
| DISTRIBUTION | Audience or dissemination used as a reason. |
| PRACTICAL_GUIDANCE | Executable steps, workflow, checklist or template. |
| ACCESSIBILITY | Explicitly free, open, low-cost or easy to access. |
| REPRODUCIBILITY | Specific conditions or materials allow others to check or repeat a result. |
| AUDIENCE_FIT | Fit for a named audience, task or constraint. |
| EVIDENCE_JUDGMENT | Limits, failures, conflicting findings or evaluation of evidence strength. |
| OTHER / UNKNOWN | Keep other concrete reasons or record that no reason can be attributed. |
