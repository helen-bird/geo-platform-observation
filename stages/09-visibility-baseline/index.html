GEO Lab #09 · AI Visibility Baseline

GEO Lab #09: How often does ChatGPT recommend my GEO work?

Helen Bird · Observed November 23–24, 2026 (Beijing time)

What appeared in the answers?

  • Mention 0/180. No answer identified Helen Bird, the project, or one of its resources in the final prose.
  • Observed citation 0/180. No recorded answer link, inline citation, or main source pointed to the target.
  • Recommendation 0/180. No answer explicitly advised readers to use, read or follow the target resources.
  • Additional sources 10/144. Two of my research pages surfaced in the “More” list.

Six questions and two thinking levels

Six fixed questions about understanding, improving and measuring GEO were each asked 15 times at Medium and High in fresh unpersonalized Temporary Chats. GPT-6 was the selected label. Search was automatic and explicitly visible in 150 answers. The remaining 30 search states are unknown.

Which of my pages surfaced?

  • Lab #04 evidence appendix appeared in 8 answers’ “More” lists.
  • Lab #06 content optimization review appeared in 3 answers’ “More” lists.

One answer surfaced both pages. The two pages appeared under the measurement-method, evidence-backed guide and beginner-learning questions. Both stayed outside the visible cited and recommended content.

What did ChatGPT recommend instead?

M2 asked for a free measurement guide or worksheet for a solo founder. My Measurement Kit was recommended in 0/30 answers and surfaced in 0/23 complete “More” lists. The answers frequently suggested other step-by-step guides and trackers.
