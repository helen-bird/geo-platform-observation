GEO Lab #09 · Supplement

GEO Lab #09 Supplement: From 0 to 16 Recommendations

Helen Bird · First retest on November 27, 2026 (Beijing time)

What changed in the answers?

I rebuilt my free GEO measurement guide and worksheet after benchmarking the resources ChatGPT recommended for the same solo-founder question. Three days after release, ChatGPT recommended my kit in 16/30 answers (53.3%), up from 0/30.

  • The leading alternatives made it easy to start. They offered an editable prompt set, a worksheet or a clear sequence of steps.
  • I rebuilt the guide and the workbook together. I also added author and evidence links and included the guide in the sitemap.
  • Both thinking levels recommended the kit after release. All 16 recommendations linked to the guide or worksheet in the answer body.

One before-and-after comparison measures a change in this batch. It cannot isolate the contribution of any edit or rule out changes in the search environment.

The original question

All 30 baseline answers and 30 first-retest answers used the same M2 question.

Can you recommend a free step-by-step guide or worksheet for measuring GEO visibility for a solo founder?

The baseline public data gave me two things: zero recommendations for my kit and a shortlist of alternatives to try. I counted a resource once per answer, read the reasons ChatGPT gave, opened the linked pages and tested the public files or workflows where I could.

T2-06. 1. Best match: GEO Lab – Free GEO Visibility Measurement Guide

T2-07. Start with GEO Lab because it is explicitly designed for solo founders and provides a downloadable Excel worksheet without requiring a paid tool. If you want a more structured competitor benchmarking exercise, use Semantic Frame next.

The kit was presented as a starting point or as one component of a workflow. Answers pointed to the solo-founder fit, the free Excel worksheet and the difference between mentions, citations and recommendations.

How to run the same kind of check

  1. Freeze one question and the target you will count. Save the full answers and score recommendations separately from links.
  2. Open the resources the answers actually recommend. Walk through a first use and record the gaps you can fix in your own product.
  3. Ship a dated release with a frozen before version. Use a separate title-only search to verify the new content is discoverable.
  4. Repeat the unchanged natural question using the original settings. Keep the results and repeat in a later window to check persistence.
