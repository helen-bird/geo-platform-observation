GEO Lab #09 · AI Visibility Baseline

GEO Lab #09: AI Visibility Methods and Evidence

Helen Bird · Observed November 23–24, 2026 (Beijing time)

Sample and attempts

180 selected answers, 182 formal submissions and two retained source-capture failures. Each failed planned position has one fresh same-condition replacement. The 12-answer pilot is outside the formal denominator. The first 39 selected positions rely on collection records for the selected settings. Later records retain direct settings evidence. GPT-6 identifies the web selector label.

Scoring rules

  • Mention. Identify the author, project or uniquely attributable asset in final prose.
  • Citation. Match saved answer links, inline citation cards and main sources to the confirmed owned routes.
  • Recommendation. Require explicit advice to use, read, follow or refer to an attributable target asset.
  • Additional sources. Count an approved target URL in the separately labelled “More” list, once per answer. Missing coverage stays unknown.

Recurring alternatives were coded from answer context and verified against verbatim evidence before URL cleanup. Lists include selected relevant resources and tools. Some answers recommend multiple resources.

An AI agent coded the answers and checked the surrounding text and links for ten potentially ambiguous target references. The project owner authorized release of this edition. The scoring record identifies the agent as the reviewer.
