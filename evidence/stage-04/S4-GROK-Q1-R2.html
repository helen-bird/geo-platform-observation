What are the best free resources to learn Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) in 2026? Include sources when available.

The agreed prompt is reproduced exactly. Any recorded difference in the actual submitted text or search UI is retained in the raw record and full appendix.

Original answer

The best free resources for learning Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) in 2026 combine foundational research, structured free courses from established SEO platforms, practitioner guides, curated roadmaps, and hands-on measurement tools. GEO focuses on getting content cited, mentioned, or recommended in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, etc.), rather than ranking in traditional link lists.

Here are the strongest free options, prioritized by practicality and authority:

Foundational Research (Start Here for Depth)
Original GEO paper: GEO: Generative Engine Optimization by Aggarwal et al. (Princeton, IIT Delhi, Georgia Tech; KDD 2024; arXiv:2311.09735). This is the primary source that defined the field. It tested 9 strategies on ~10,000 queries and found lifts of roughly +41% from expert quotations, +33% from statistics, +29% from fluency, and +28% from citing sources, while keyword stuffing hurt performance. Free on arXiv.⁠Geoaura
Follow-up work such as the 2025 comparative study across major engines (arXiv:2509.08919) and related benchmarks.⁠Thegeocommunity

These give the empirical basis most courses build on.

Top Free Structured Courses (1–2.5 hours each)
Ahrefs Academy AEO Course (ahrefs.com/academy/aeo-course): ~1 hour 26 minutes, 12 lessons. Covers how AI search works, platform differences (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), content that gets cited, brand mentions, technical practices, and measurement. No signup required for the course; highly data-driven. Optional certification exam needs a free Ahrefs account.⁠Ahrefs
Apollo Digital Free GEO Course (apollodigital.io/learn/geo/): ~100 minutes, 7 lessons. Foundations, technical readiness/crawlers, measurement (with free Google Sheets templates for audits and share-of-voice), and tooling. Fully free, no signup, progress saves in-browser; uses only free tools.⁠Apollodigital
Rank Prompt GEO & AEO Course (rankprompt.com/geo-course/): ~90 minutes, 20 lessons. Strong for agencies/consultants—covers internal/external GEO, schema, llms.txt, crawlability, citation building, and packaging/selling the service. Templates included; no certificate.⁠Rankprompt
HubSpot Academy AEO Fundamentals: ~2 hours 20 minutes with a free certificate. Solid beginner foundations on AI-powered answers, E-E-A-T, content planning, and measurement.
Surfer Academy AI Search Optimization: ~2 hours with an exam-based certificate (valid ~2 years). Focuses on AI-ready content, topical authority, schema, and workflows.
AISQ Growth GEO Academy (aisq.com/learn-geo/): Free short lessons with worksheets/templates; practical action-plan focus.
Finseo Academy YouTube playlist: Free multi-lesson series on AEO/GEO metrics, entities, tracking, and actions.

Other free academy options exist from Semrush, seoClarity, and Muck Rack (the latter more PR-oriented).

Curated Roadmaps and Practitioner Guides
LearningAIsearch.com by Aleyda Solis: Free, non-biased roadmap specifically for AI search optimization (GEO/AEO/LLMO). Sections cover fundamentals, measurement/KPIs, ecommerce/agentic commerce, tools, content checklists, and technical aspects. Complements her broader LearningSEO.io resource. Excellent for structured self-study without ads or paid bias.⁠Learningaisearch
The GEO Lab free ebooks/guides (thegeolab.net): Pocket Guide (2026 edition, ~30 pages, beginner-friendly with checklists and 30-day plan), SEO-to-GEO framework, experiments guide, and others. Direct downloads or free via Gumroad (price $0).
The GEO Community (thegeocommunity.com): Blog with practitioner experiments (crawlability, schema, LLMs.txt, entities, measurement), plus a structured /start learning path with role-based tracks (Strategist, Builder, Engineer).
Practitioner blogs/newsletters from active SEO experts (e.g., Aleyda Solis’s SEOFOMO, Neil Patel guides, and others sharing citation data) and Google Search Central documentation on AI Overviews, AI Mode, and structured data.
Practical Free Tools and Measurement
Google Search Console (includes Generative AI performance reports added around mid-2026) + GA4 for tracking AI visibility and traffic.
Free tiers of platforms like Ahrefs for basic brand/gap analysis.
Browser-based AI tools themselves for manual prompt testing and competitor analysis.
Open checklists, Google Sheets templates (from courses above), and community Discord/Reddit/Slack groups for peer feedback.
Recommended Learning Path (2026)
Read the original GEO paper + skim Google’s official AI search docs.
Take the Ahrefs AEO course + Apollo Digital or Rank Prompt course for structure and templates.
Follow LearningAIsearch.com roadmap and The GEO Lab pocket guide.
Apply to 3–5 pages on a site you control, measure citations over 4–8 weeks using Search Console + manual prompt tracking, and iterate via communities/practitioner blogs.

Notes: Many “GEO” courses emphasize AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) interchangeably. Prioritize resources backed by data or active practitioners over pure theory. Certificates (HubSpot, Surfer, etc.) are useful signals but less important than a portfolio of measured results. The field evolves quickly—pair static courses with ongoing practitioner content and official platform updates.
