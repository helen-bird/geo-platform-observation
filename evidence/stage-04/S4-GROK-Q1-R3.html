Original prompt

What are the best free resources to learn Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) in 2026? Include sources when available.

The agreed prompt is reproduced exactly. Any recorded difference in the actual submitted text or search UI is retained in the raw record and full appendix.

Original answer

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing content, technical setup, entities, and authority signals so AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, etc.) cite, mention, or recommend your brand/pages in generated answers. It builds on SEO foundations but focuses on extractable passages, brand mentions, crawl access for AI bots, and measurement via citations rather than rankings/clicks.⁠Thegeocommunity

As of late 2026, the strongest free resources come from the original research, practitioner-built courses, curated roadmaps, and official platform docs. Here’s a prioritized list of the best ones.

Foundational Research (Start Here for Depth)
“GEO: Generative Engine Optimization” (Aggarwal et al., Princeton/IIT Delhi, published at KDD 2024): The primary source paper. It tested optimization strategies across thousands of queries and found evidence-based tactics (citations, statistics, quotations) can raise visibility/citation rates by roughly 30–40%. Free on arXiv: arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735. Related follow-up papers exist on cross-engine behavior.⁠Thegeocommunity
Structured Free Courses (Practical, Self-Paced)

These are among the most recommended practical starting points:

Apollo Digital Free GEO Course — ~7 lessons / ~100 minutes of reading. Covers foundations (what changed vs. SEO), getting cited by ChatGPT, AI crawler access (and myths like llms.txt), measurement (AI Visibility Audit + Share of Voice), and tooling. Includes free Google Sheets templates; no signup required; progress saves in the browser. Runs entirely on free tools. apollodigital.io/learn/geo (updated Aug 2026).⁠Apollodigital
Ahrefs Academy Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Course — 12 lessons / ~1h 26m. Explains how AI search works across platforms, prompt/keyword research, content that gets cited, brand mentions, technical practices, and measurement. No signup required for the course itself (exam needs an Ahrefs Free account). Strong data backing from large-scale studies. ahrefs.com/academy/aeo-course.⁠Ahrefs
Rank Prompt Free GEO & AEO Course — ~90 minutes / 20 lessons. Framework-focused (internal vs. external GEO), on-site optimization, and practical for agencies/consultants. Includes templates and SOPs; free with no card required. rankprompt.com/geo-course.⁠Rankprompt
AISQ Growth GEO Academy — Free 5-lesson course with worksheets/templates. Walks from awareness to an action plan for visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI features. aisq.com/learn-geo.⁠Aisq

Other solid free options with certificates (or exams) include HubSpot Academy (AEO/AI marketing modules), Surfer Academy, and Semrush Academy AI Search Optimization courses.

Curated Learning Paths & Practitioner Hubs
LearningAIsearch.com (by Aleyda Solis) — Free, non-commercial roadmap that aggregates reliable free guides across fundamentals, technical setup, content optimization, measurement, brand authority/citations, and tools. Continuously updated; designed to avoid hype. Excellent structured entry point. learningaisearch.com.⁠Learningaisearch
The GEO Community — Highly active practitioner blog and resources in 2026 covering crawlability, schema, LLMs.txt, log analysis, entity strategy, experiments, and engine-specific behavior. Includes a role-based /start learning path (Strategist, Builder, Engineer). Frequently cited as one of the best free open foundations. thegeocommunity.com (see their resources/courses page and start path).⁠Thegeocommunity
The GEO Lab Free Ebooks — Multiple free guides built around their GEO Stack framework. Start with the GEO Pocket Guide — 2026 Edition (quick fundamentals, extractability, checklists). Others cover full frameworks, experiments, field manuals, and more. Available for free download. thegeolab.net/ebooks.⁠Thegeolab
Official Platform Documentation
Google Search Central:
AI features and your website
Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search
Google stresses that foundational SEO (indexing, helpful people-first content, technical requirements for snippets) is what matters for AI Overviews and AI Mode; no special AI-only files or markup are required beyond normal best practices. Search Console also gained generative AI performance reporting in 2026.⁠Developers.google

Platform crawler docs (OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic, Bing/Copilot) are also free and essential for technical access.

Recommended Free Learning Path
Read the original arXiv paper for the empirical foundation.
Complete one structured course (Apollo Digital or Ahrefs for speed and practicality).
Follow LearningAIsearch.com + The GEO Community for ongoing, practitioner depth and experiments.
Download the GEO Lab Pocket Guide for a concise checklist.
Apply immediately: Run baseline prompts (signed out) on your category in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Mode, use free templates for an AI visibility audit, ensure AI crawlers can access your site, and measure changes.
Cross-check with Google’s official docs and solid free SEO fundamentals (Ahrefs free SEO courses or LearningSEO.io), since GEO layers on top of them.

Notes: Free resources from active practitioners (those sharing real citation data) are generally more valuable than theoretical credential programs. The field evolves quickly, so prioritize recent material (2025–2026) and hands-on measurement over theory alone. Communities, newsletters from trusted SEOs, and your own experiments are key for staying current.

These should give you a strong, practical start without cost.
