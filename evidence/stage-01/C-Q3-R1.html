Original prompt

Who publishes practical, data-backed GEO tutorials for independent builders? Explain why and include sources when available.

The agreed prompt is reproduced exactly. Any recorded difference in the actual submitted text or search UI is retained in the raw record and full appendix.

Original answer

For an independent builder, I would prioritize GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) resources that teach you to establish a baseline, run a small experiment, measure AI mentions and citations, and decide what to change next. Free worksheets and reproducible research are more useful than generic lists of ranking tips.

My strongest starting points are Aleyda Solís for practical workflows, Ahrefs for structured tutorials and large-scale data, and MLforSEO for an evidence-labeled checklist. Seer Interactive and Search Engine Land are useful when you want to learn how practitioners test specific GEO hypotheses.

3. An important qualification about GEO evidence

A tutorial can be data-backed without proving that its recommended tactic reliably causes more AI mentions.

Two findings illustrate the distinction:

A study reported by Axios on October 28, 2026, found that repeated ChatGPT queries on the same day shared only 41% of cited domains. A single before-and-after observation can therefore be misleading. 
Axios

A 2026 research survey covering 45 GEO studies concluded that evidence for stable, long-term, cross-platform causal improvements in organic discoverability remains limited. 
arXiv

When evaluating any tutorial, look for four things: a defined baseline, a repeatable protocol, an appropriate comparison or control, and results measured across multiple runs. Treat large correlation studies as evidence for hypotheses, not proof that a specific intervention will work for your account.
