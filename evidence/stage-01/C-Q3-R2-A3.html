Original answer

Who publishes the best practical, data-backed GEO tutorials for independent builders?

As of October 2026, I would prioritize independent SEO researchers, experimental marketing teams, and practitioners who publish reproducible experiments over generic GEO agencies.

There is an important distinction between three types of content:

Experimental research: Tests whether a particular GEO tactic actually changes AI visibility.

Implementation tutorials: Shows how to execute GEO improvements on a real website or personal brand.

Industry analysis: Explains how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews discover and recommend sources.

For an independent builder, the ideal learning source combines the first two.

1. My recommended publishers

1. Seer Interactive — Best for controlled experiments

Highest priority

Research team / SEO agency · Free research

Publishes experiments testing individual GEO variables.

Focuses on whether changes to content, author information, schema, freshness and page structure affect AI visibility.

Its experimental approach is useful because it attempts to isolate individual factors instead of changing everything simultaneously.

Why follow: This is closest to the scientific method. You can reproduce its experiments on your own website.

Start with: Seer Interactive experiments and case studies. The article describes its AI Lab and controlled testing methodology.

2. Ahrefs — Best for large-scale quantitative research

Highest priority

SEO software company · Extensive free research

Analyzed 75,000 brands across millions of AI responses.

Examines relationships between brand mentions, YouTube presence, backlinks and AI visibility.

Publishes practical guides on monitoring ChatGPT mentions and identifying third-party sources.

Why follow: Its research provides useful quantitative evidence and actionable ideas for content distribution.

Recommended reading:

Top Brand Visibility Factors in ChatGPT, AI Mode, and AI Overviews — 75,000 brands.

How to Monitor Brand Mentions in ChatGPT — includes a free manual monitoring approach.

AI Search Strategy: 4 Pillars to Show Up More — practical strategy.

Important limitation: Most of its large-scale findings are correlations, not causal experiments.

3. Kevin Indig / Growth Memo — Best for understanding AI recommendations

Highest priority

Independent growth researcher · Newsletter and research

Studies how brands appear in AI answers and how users respond to recommendations.

Distinguishes brand mentions, citations, visibility and actual business outcomes.

Collaborates with Semrush on large-scale research.

Why follow: Many GEO practitioners optimize for citations without understanding whether AI actually recommends their brand. Kevin's work addresses this distinction.

Recommended reading:

Industry publication · Free articles

Publishes practitioner-led experiments, technical analyses and case studies.

5. One important research principle

GEO is still an evolving field. A lot of published advice is based on correlations, small case studies or vendor-reported results.

For example:

Ahrefs' research involving 75,000 brands identifies strong correlations between brand mentions and AI visibility. It cannot establish that deliberately increasing mentions will cause higher visibility. 
Ahrefs

The Princeton study demonstrates improvements under controlled experimental conditions, with limitations when transferring findings to live AI search systems. 
数字对象标识符

Recent academic reviews also highlight substantial variation across platforms, prompts and measurement methods. 
arXiv

I would use the following evidence hierarchy when evaluating a GEO tutorial:

Controlled experiments with control groups.

Repeated experiments with disclosed datasets and methodology.

Large-scale observational research.

Case studies with baseline, timeframe and measurable outcomes.

Practitioner opinions and unverified recommendations.

My overall recommendation: Build your learning around Aleyda + Ahrefs + Seer, and use Kevin Indig to connect the technical work with business outcomes.
