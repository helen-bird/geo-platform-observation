Original answer

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing content, entities, and technical signals so AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, etc.) cite, mention, or recommend your brand/pages in synthesized answers rather than just ranking blue links. It overlaps heavily with Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and traditional SEO fundamentals (crawlability, E-E-A-T, clear structure, authority).

Here are the strongest free resources available as of late 2026, prioritized for practical value from active practitioners and official sources.

Structured Free Courses (Best Starting Points)

These offer the fastest path from fundamentals to measurement:

Apollo Digital Free GEO Course — 7 lessons (~100 minutes) covering foundations, ChatGPT citations, technical readiness (robots.txt), measurement loops, and free Google Sheets templates (visibility audit + share-of-voice tracker). No signup required; progress saves in the browser. Runs entirely on free tools.⁠Apollodigital
Rank Prompt Free GEO & AEO Course — ~20 lessons (~90 minutes). Covers the GEO framework (internal vs external), on-site optimization (schema, crawlability, llms.txt context), off-site citations, and packaging GEO as a service. Free with templates/SOPs.⁠Rankprompt
Ahrefs Academy Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Course — 12 lessons (1 hour 26 minutes). Explains how AI search works, platform differences (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity), brand gap analysis, content that gets cited, technical practices, and measurement. No signup required.⁠Ahrefs
HubSpot Academy AEO Fundamentals — Free certification course (~2 hours 20 minutes, 19 videos). Covers how answer engines select content, E-E-A-T, content structure for citations, and measurement. Account required but free.⁠Academy.hubspot
AISQ Growth GEO Academy — Free standalone course (5 lessons + worksheets/templates) focused on getting recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. No paywall.⁠Aisq
Botric GEO Foundations — Free practical modules (several hours of content available, more rolling out) on GEO vs SEO, how LLMs/RAG work, citation gaps, and the four pillars of AI visibility.⁠Botric

Other short free options appear on platforms like Udemy (search “GEO” or “AEO GEO 2026”) and Open University-style intros listed on Class Central.

Curated Learning Paths and Aggregators
LearningAIsearch.com (by Aleyda Solis) — Free, non-biased roadmap aggregating reliable free guides across fundamentals, technical setup, content, brand authority/citations, measurement, prompt research, and e-commerce/agentic commerce. Continuously updated; no ads or paid placements.⁠Learningaisearch
The GEO Community — Practitioner blog with experiment-backed guides (crawlability, schema, entity strategy, log analysis) plus a structured /start learning path with role-based tracks (Strategist, Builder, Engineer). Strong emphasis on live experiments and documented results.⁠Thegeocommunity
Class Central’s “25 Resources to Learn Generative Engine Optimization in 2026” — Roundup of free courses, blogs, tools, podcasts, and YouTube channels, with beginner-friendly recommendations.⁠Classcentral
Stackmatix free AEO/GEO resources overview — Comparison of resource types plus a practical 4-phase learning path (fundamentals → competitive landscape → tactical frameworks → apply & measure).⁠Stackmatix
Foundational Research (Read These Early)
Original GEO paper: “GEO: Generative Engine Optimization” by Aggarwal et al. (Princeton/IIT Delhi, KDD 2024, arXiv:2311.09735). Defines the problem, tests strategies (e.g., statistics, expert quotes, fluency, citations) across thousands of queries, and reports substantial citation lifts. Primary source for the field.⁠Thegeocommunity
Follow-up comparative studies (e.g., across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) and related work such as AutoGEO frameworks.

Manual testing: Run the same prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI experiences and track mentions.
Recommended Learning Path (Self-Study)
Week 1: Read the original arXiv GEO paper + one short course (Ahrefs AEO or Apollo Digital) for foundations.
Weeks 2–3: Work through LearningAIsearch.com or The GEO Community /start path; complete HubSpot or Rank Prompt for structure and measurement.
Ongoing: Apply tactics (lead with clear, quotable answers; add statistics/sources; strengthen entity signals and crawlability) on 3–5 pages. Measure with free tools + manual prompt testing. Supplement with practitioner blogs and the GEO Lab pocket guide.
Track results and iterate—real citation data from your own site is the best feedback.

GEO evolves quickly with AI platform changes, so prioritize recent practitioner content and primary research over generic theory. Combine one structured course with the Aleyda Solis roadmap or GEO Community materials for the most efficient free path. Official Google/Bing documentation provides the authoritative baseline for their ecosystems.
