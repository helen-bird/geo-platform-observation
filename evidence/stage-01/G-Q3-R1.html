Who publishes practical, data-backed GEO tutorials for independent builders? Explain why and include sources when available.

Original answer

The GEO Lab (Artur Ferreira) and practitioners like Sam Shev stand out for practical, data-backed GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tutorials suited to independent builders.⁠Thegeolab

GEO focuses on getting content cited in AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and similar systems, rather than traditional search rankings.

The GEO Lab (thegeolab.net)

Artur Ferreira’s GEO Lab publishes a free library of practitioner resources, including the GEO Field Manual (a comprehensive working guide), Pocket Guide, GEO Experiments, 30-day Workbook, and related materials. These center on the five-layer GEO Stack (Retrieval Probability, Extractability, Entity Reinforcement, Structural Authority, and System Memory).⁠Thegeolab

They emphasize:

Diagnostic audits and section-level scoring.
Experiment templates and measurement (including their own tests, e.g., declarative structures outperforming narrative ones in citation rates).
Practical implementation: answer-first openings, extractable chunks, schema, entity consistency, and 30-day starter roadmaps.
References to primary research, including the foundational Princeton/IIT Delhi KDD 2024 paper.

These are free for personal and commercial use, structured as reference tools rather than pure theory, and suitable for site owners or solo builders who want measurable, experiment-driven work without agency budgets.

Sam Shev and similar roadmaps

Sam Shev (fractional CMO focused on early-stage SaaS) published a detailed 90-Day Generative Engine Optimization Roadmap. It is explicitly grounded in the Aggarwal et al. (KDD 2024 / arXiv:2311.09735) study that tested content techniques across ~10,000 queries and found lifts from adding quotations (~+40%), statistics, fluency/clarity, and inline citations, with larger relative gains often going to lower-ranked or smaller sites. Keyword stuffing performed worse than baseline.⁠Samshev

The roadmap breaks into phases:

Baseline prompt audits (25–50 buyer questions across AI tools).
Technical fixes (crawler access, schema).
Rewrites of existing high-value pages using evidence-based techniques (Answer Capsule style, named quotes, stats with sources).
Content production, off-site signals, and KPI tracking (citation rate, AI share of voice, referral traffic).

It prioritizes maximizing existing content equity and repeatable processes, which fits independent or lean teams.

Other practical options
Apollo Digital offers a free ~100-minute GEO course with lessons on foundations, crawler access, measurement (including free Google Sheets audit templates), and tooling.
Julian Goldie and similar creators share free step-by-step systems using tools like Search Console exports plus free AI agents.
Indie-oriented pieces appear on sites like Indietool, HeyCatch, Solo Business Hub, and Indie Hackers, often covering 30-day plans, entity consistency, and low-cost distribution for solo founders.
Why these work for independent builders

The academic foundation (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024) showed that substance—quotes, statistics, and citations—improves visibility inside AI answers more reliably than classic keyword tactics, and relative gains can be larger for smaller or mid-ranked sites. The best tutorials translate this into low-resource actions: manual prompt baselines, answer-first rewrites, schema markup, extractable structure, entity consistency across the web, and simple before/after measurement. They avoid heavy tool dependency at the start and focus on what independent builders can ship themselves.⁠Aisearch

Start with the GEO Lab Field Manual or Pocket Guide for a full framework, or Sam Shev’s roadmap for a concrete 90-day sequence. Always run your own baseline prompt tests, as AI citation behavior evolves. Primary source for the key numbers remains the 2024 KDD paper (arXiv:2311.09735).
