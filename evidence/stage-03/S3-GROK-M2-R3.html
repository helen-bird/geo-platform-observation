Original prompt

As a bootstrapped SaaS builder, what's a free, repeatable workflow for checking whether my product appears in ChatGPT and Grok recommendations?

The agreed prompt is reproduced exactly. Any recorded difference in the actual submitted text or search UI is retained in the raw record and full appendix.

Original answer

Here’s a free, repeatable workflow that bootstrapped SaaS founders actually use to check whether ChatGPT and Grok recommend their product.⁠01net

It takes ~30–45 minutes once set up, then 20–40 minutes on a weekly or bi-weekly cadence. Total cost: $0.

1. One-time setup (30–60 min)

Define 8–15 buyer prompts (the questions real prospects ask, not your brand name):

Category: “best [your category] for [audience/use case]”
Alternatives: “best alternatives to [top competitor]”
Problem: “tools to [problem your product solves]”
Comparison: “[competitor] vs [other competitor]”
Direct (secondary): “what is [your product]” / “is [your product] worth it”

Create a simple Google Sheet with columns:

Date | Prompt | Platform | Mentioned (Y/N) | Position (if listed) | Sentiment | Competitors named | Sources cited | Notes/quote

Run free snapshot tools once for a baseline:

ranked.ai free checker — live answers from ChatGPT, Grok, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Llama (no signup). Paste one of your prompts + domain.
Snoika brand checker — includes Grok; distinguishes “knows you” vs “recommends you” + community/trust signals.
Indexly Grok Visibility Checker — free, no login, paste your URL.
Ahrefs AI Visibility Checker (brand name, no signup) for a broader ChatGPT-heavy view.
2. Repeatable routine (weekly or every 2 weeks)
Clean sessions only
ChatGPT: Temporary chat or incognito + web search enabled.
Grok: Fresh chat on grok.x.ai (or the X app). Grok pulls real-time web + X data, so results can shift faster.
Run your core prompts (start with your top 5–8). Do each once or twice — answers are non-deterministic.
Log results immediately in the sheet. Note exact wording when you appear and which competitors/sources win instead.
Optional free automation layer
Set up Mangools AI Search Watcher free plan (5 prompts, 1 monitor, weekly updates). It covers ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Claude, AI Overviews, and others. Perfect for a small recurring sample without manual work every week.
Monthly deeper look
Re-run the free snapshot tools, check “yourbrand -site:yourdomain.com” on Google for third-party mentions, and glance at G2/Capterra/Reddit/Product Hunt presence. Those sources heavily influence both ChatGPT and Grok recommendations.⁠Amicited
What the data usually shows
Recommendation visibility is driven more by third-party mentions (reviews, Reddit, comparison listicles, community discussion) than by your own blog alone.
ChatGPT and Grok often cite different sources; tracking both is worthwhile.
One check is noisy. Trends over 4–8 weeks are what matter.
Quick improvement loop (informed by the checks)

When you’re missing from answers, the usual high-leverage free/low-cost fixes are:

Honest comparison / alternatives pages on your site
More genuine reviews on G2/Capterra
Useful answers in relevant Reddit threads (disclose you’re the founder)
Clear, structured product pages that answer buyer questions directly

This workflow stays free, stays repeatable, and gives you the same signal paid GEO tools charge for — just with a bit of discipline on the logging side. Start with the prompt list and the Google Sheet this week; the rest compounds from there.
